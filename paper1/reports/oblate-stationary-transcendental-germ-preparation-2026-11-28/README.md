## Why an auxiliary rational value suffices

Choose $r_0\in\mathbb Q_{>0}$ in the physical radial domain. Write $q=1/(10r_0)$ and $T_0=\arctan q$ on the real branch. Since $q$ is positive rational, it is algebraic and belongs to none of $\{0,i,-i\}$. Manuel Eberl's formalization gives the arctangent result directly in `transcendental_Arctan`. Equivalently, $e^{2iT_0}=(1+iq)/(1-iq)$ is algebraic. If $T_0$ were algebraic, $2iT_0$ would be nonzero algebraic, and `algebraic_exp_complex_iff` would make this exponential transcendental. [Eberl, Hermite–Lindemann, §7.6 and the exponential corollary](https://isa-afp.org/browser_info/current/AFP/Hermite_Lindemann/Hermite_Lindemann.html)

It follows that every nonzero polynomial in $\mathbb Q[T]$ is nonzero at $T_0$. For any required polynomial $P(r,T)$, exhibiting $P(r_0,t_*)\ne0$ at a rational auxiliary $t_*$ proves that $P(r_0,T)$ is a nonzero polynomial and hence $P(r_0,T_0)\ne0$. This argument applies **after** radial specialization. A nonzero polynomial in two variables can specialize to the zero polynomial at a particular $r_0$. Checking all required denominators at the same pair makes specialization of every rational expression used below well-defined.

## From nonvanishing to the germ

Analyticity of $T(r)$ and continuity give a common open interval containing $r_0$ on which all required denominators and the minor remain nonzero. On that interval $JM=I_8$ and $CM=0$ imply $\operatorname{im}M=\ker C$, since $\operatorname{rank}C=6$ in fourteen state coordinates. Consequently $SM=0$ implies $S=LC$ with $L=S_{:,P}(C_{:,P})^{-1}$ analytic. Solve $z'=Kz$ with the physical initial datum above. The violation $h=Cz$ obeys $h'=Lh$, $h(r_0)=0$, so uniqueness gives $Cz=0$ along the analytic germ. The lift then gives $v'=w$, and all 22 retained equations hold. The physical discarded response therefore satisfies $D_{\mathrm{disc}}(r_0)=d(r_0,T_0)z(r_0)=(dM)_j(r_0,T_0)\ne0$.

## Failure cases and scope

If $P(r_0,T)\equiv0$, every dummy value gives zero at that radius. This can be established by checking all univariate coefficients. If $P(r_0,T)$ is nonzero but $P(r_0,t_*)=0$, only this auxiliary evaluation failed. For example, $r-a$ vanishes on the entire specialization $r_0=a$, whereas $T-b$ vanishes at the single dummy value $t_*=b$. Either a zero denominator or a zero determinant disqualifies the proposed pair for that proof. A zero response entry disqualifies it as a witness for that column. Bounded search exhaustion leaves the physical witness unresolved. Even an identically zero response row on this forward family would require a separate converse to cover all original stationary germs.

Exact signs and magnitudes in the replay record belong to the auxiliary rational evaluation. Physical sign, magnitude and numerical interval width lie outside this method's conclusion, as do a global boundary-domain result, universal silence, nonlinear stabilization and a physical degree-of-freedom count.
